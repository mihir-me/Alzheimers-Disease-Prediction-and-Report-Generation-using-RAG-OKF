# NMDA Receptor Antagonist

Memantine is approved for moderate-to-severe AD and is often used in
combination with a cholinesterase inhibitor. It modulates glutamatergic
signaling and may delay functional decline.

Related: [Dementia Staging](/overview/clinical_stages_cdr.md).
