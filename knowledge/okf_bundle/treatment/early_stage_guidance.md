# When the Model Predicts Early-Stage Disease

If the model flags Very Mild Impairment (MCI-like), appropriate next steps
include formal cognitive assessment, vascular risk optimization, monitoring of
progression, and discussion of early intervention options with a specialist.

Related: [Dementia Staging](/overview/clinical_stages_cdr.md), [MMSE](/scores/mmse.md), [CDR Scale](/scores/cdr_scale.md).
