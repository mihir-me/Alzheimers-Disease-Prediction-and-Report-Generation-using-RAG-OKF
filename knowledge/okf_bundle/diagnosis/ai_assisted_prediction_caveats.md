# Caveats for AI-Assisted Prediction

An automated prediction from imaging and clinical scores is a decision-support
tool, not a definitive diagnosis. It should always be interpreted in the full
clinical context and confirmed by a qualified clinician. Imaging-only
classifications can be affected by image quality, slice position, and the
patient's age and head size.
