# Typical Diagnostic Workup

1. History and collateral information (usually from a family member).
2. Cognitive screening: MMSE, MoCA.
3. Functional assessment: ability to perform activities of daily living.
4. Clinical staging: CDR scale.
5. Structural neuroimaging: MRI to assess hippocampal/whole-brain atrophy and
   exclude other causes (tumors, vascular disease, normal pressure
   hydrocephalus).
6. Laboratory tests to rule out reversible causes (thyroid, B12, infection).
7. Genetic testing for APOE-epsilon4 when relevant.
