# NIA-AA Diagnostic Framework (2011 / 2018)

The National Institute on Aging and Alzheimer's Association (NIA-AA) framework
recognizes Alzheimer's disease as a biological continuum:

- **Preclinical AD:** biomarker evidence of amyloid/tau pathology without
  clinical symptoms.
- **MCI due to AD:** objective cognitive impairment with preserved daily
  function plus biomarker evidence.
- **AD dementia:** objective cognitive impairment that interferes with daily
  activities plus biomarker evidence.

A diagnosis is probabilistic and multidisciplinary: it combines history from the
patient and an informant, cognitive testing, functional assessment, physical and
neurological examination, laboratory workup, and neuroimaging.
